Promotion gate
1. Incident commander identifies recovery authority.
2. Old primary + workers fenced; independently verify.
3. Record durable/replayed WAL positions and available archives.
4. Calculate exposure; obtain explicit RPO exception if required.
5. Promote once; update API, workers and connection pools.
6. Verify single writer and reconcile exposed business IDs.
7. Preserve divergent old data; rebuild replica before planned failback.
